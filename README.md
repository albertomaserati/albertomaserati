## Hi, I'm Alberto 👋

Engineering Manager based in Pavia, Italy. I've been writing C# since 2014. In 2019 I co-founded BindingFuture and led its technology as the company grew to 35 people, until its acquisition by Teoresi Group, where I now lead a 20-engineer unit working on .NET, web and mobile.

My day job is software architecture and people: setting technical direction, reviewing architecture decisions, hiring and growing engineers. Along the way I've worked on a microservices platform designed with Domain-Driven Design for Flowe (Banca Mediolanum's digital bank) and on DINA, a SaaS platform for smart building management.

I still write code in my spare time. That's what you'll find here.
